A typical itinerary would look like this

  1. From JHB we head east to Mpumalanga to the scenic town of Graskop where we will stay for a day or 2

  2. Explore the scenic area around Graskop and the waterfalls, places of interest and much more

  3. Time to travel down the escarpment to the Lowveld to enter the Kruger at Orpen Gate. On route we will stop at some of the most scenic viewpoints like the 3 sisters with spectacular views over the Blyde River Canyon.

  4. Time to find the Big 5 in Kruger and enjoy the most beautiful sunsets Africa has to offer

  5. Some more game viewing on the way to our next rest camp. An African Braai that evening will put the cherry on top

  6. Last morning to view some animals before leaving the park at Phabeni gate. Through Hazyview we head back to Graskop for a last night in Africa before traveling back to JHB

  7. Travelling back to JHB

The following tours will follow the same basic route, with additional golf and adventure specific activities

An itinerary for a private specialised golf tour would look like this

  1. Visit a golf shop, get used to SA conditions and go play Kimiad Golf course in Pretoria to get rid of the Jet lag.

  2. Play an estate course in Gauteng like Blair Atholl (no 6 on the Top Courses in SA) or even Elements (no10) or the well known Royal Johannesburg before heading east to Mpumalanga.

  3. Next stop Dullstroom to play the Ernie Els signature course with its spectacular vistas.

  4. Travel to Graskop for a one night stay in a luxurious guest house

  5. Enter the Kruger at Orpen gate for two very special days in the game reserve.

  6. Before leaving the Kruger we will have one more round at Skukuza Golf Course, an absolute must!

  7. Travelling back to JNB

Design your own tour

None of the tours above are set in stone. You can also design your own tour in collaboration with us. Tell us what you would like to see and do, and we will shape the route, the pace and the activities around you.

  1. Share your wishes

    Golf, game viewing, scenic drives, adventure or a mix of everything. Tell us who is travelling and for how long, from 4 to 20 days.

  2. We plan it together

    We can help you plan your trip, or fit in with an itinerary you already have, and add the secret places only the locals know about.

  3. Sit back and enjoy

    Once the plan is agreed, Elardus takes you to all your destinations, as safe and sound as possible.
